# Awesome AI Advertising [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Curated tools, models, and resources for creating and optimizing ads with AI.

AI is changing how ads get made: creative generation, UGC-style video, copywriting, and campaign optimization are all being automated. This list collects the tools and resources that are actually useful for performance marketers, ecommerce brands, and agencies.

Maintained by [AdRiseLab](https://adriselab.com) — AI ad creative and performance marketing automation for Meta Ads.

## Creative Generation

Tools that generate static ad creatives, product visuals, and brand imagery.

- [AdCreative.ai](https://adcreative.ai) - Generates conversion-focused ad creatives and banners in brand style.
- [AdRiseLab](https://adriselab.com/product/creative-generation) - Turns product photos into ready-to-run AI ad creatives for Meta Ads, built for performance marketers.
- [Canva Magic Studio](https://www.canva.com/magic/) - AI design suite for generating and editing marketing visuals.
- [Flair](https://flair.ai) - AI product photography with branded scenes and templates.
- [Icon](https://icon.com) - AI admaker that generates, scripts, and edits ads end to end.
- [Omneky](https://omneky.com) - Generates personalized ad creatives at scale using LLMs and vision models.
- [Pencil](https://www.trypencil.com) - AI ad generation platform for ecommerce and agencies with performance predictions.
- [Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com) - AI photo editing for product shots, backgrounds, and batch creative production.
- [Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai) - AI image generation and editing with strong brand style and vector support.

## Video Ads & UGC

AI avatars, UGC-style video ads, and generative video for paid social.

- [Arcads](https://arcads.ai) - Creates UGC-style video ads with AI actors from a text script.
- [Captions](https://www.captions.ai) - AI video editing and talking-head video creation optimized for short-form.
- [Creatify](https://creatify.ai) - Generates video ads and AI avatar UGC from a product URL.
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com) - AI avatar video generation with multilingual support and API access.
- [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai) - Cinematic AI video generation with motion and camera controls.
- [Kling](https://klingai.com) - High-quality generative video model for realistic product and lifestyle footage.
- [Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai) - Generative video model for fast, realistic clips from text or images.
- [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro) - Repurposes long-form video into short clips scored for virality.
- [Pika](https://pika.art) - Text-to-video and image-to-video generation for short creative clips.
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com) - Generative video creation and editing suite used for commercial production.
- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io) - AI avatar video platform for polished presenter-style videos.

## Ad Intelligence & Inspiration

Ad libraries and research tools for studying what competitors run.

- [AdMapix](https://www.admapix.com) - Searches competitor ad creatives across Meta, TikTok, Google, and other channels, with AI analysis and research reports.
- [Foreplay](https://www.foreplay.co) - Saves, organizes, and briefs ads from Meta and TikTok ad libraries.
- [Google Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com) - Official library of ads running across Google surfaces.
- [MagicBrief](https://magicbrief.com) - Ad inspiration library and creative analytics for performance teams.
- [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) - Official searchable database of every ad running on Meta platforms.
- [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/pc/en) - Top-performing TikTok ads, trends, and audio by region and industry.

## APIs & Models

Generative media APIs and models for building your own ad creative pipelines.

- [Black Forest Labs](https://bfl.ai) - FLUX image models with strong prompt adherence and text rendering.
- [fal.ai](https://fal.ai) - Fast inference API for image and video generation models.
- [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai) - Image generation with reliable text rendering, useful for ad headlines in-image.
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com) - High-aesthetic image generation for concept and lifestyle visuals.
- [Replicate](https://replicate.com) - Run and fine-tune open-source image and video models via API.
- [Stability AI](https://stability.ai) - Open image, video, and audio models including Stable Diffusion.
