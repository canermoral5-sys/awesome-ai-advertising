# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping make this list better! Please follow these guidelines when suggesting additions.

## What belongs here

- Tools, models, or resources that use AI for **advertising**: creative generation, video ads, ad copy, campaign optimization, or ad research.
- Actively maintained products with a working website.
- Things you (or people you trust) have actually used and found valuable.

## What doesn't belong here

- General-purpose AI tools with no clear advertising use case.
- Abandoned projects or waitlist-only landing pages.
- Thin wrappers with no meaningful functionality of their own.

## How to add an entry

1. Fork this repository and create a branch.
2. Add your entry in the relevant category, **in alphabetical order**.
3. Use this format: `- [Name](https://example.com) - One-sentence description ending with a period.`
4. Keep the description factual and neutral — describe what it does, not marketing claims.
5. One entry per pull request, with a short note on why it deserves inclusion.

## Quality checks

Before submitting, make sure:

- The link works and points to the tool's main page (no affiliate or UTM parameters).
- The description is a single sentence, starts with a capital letter, and ends with a period.
- The entry is not already on the list.
